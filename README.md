# Cyclistic Bike-Share Analysis

## Business Problem
Cyclistic is a bike-share company that wants to increase the number of annual memberships. 
This project analyzes how **casual riders** and **annual members** use Cyclistic bikes differently 
to identify opportunities to convert casual riders into annual members.

## Analysis Process
1. Imported and combined multiple monthly datasets
2. Cleaned data by removing duplicates and invalid ride durations
3. Created new variables (ride length, day of week)
4. Compared usage patterns between casual riders and annual members
5. Visualized trends to support findings

---

## Key Insights
- Casual riders take longer rides on average than annual members
- Annual members ride more frequently on weekdays
- Casual riders show higher usage on weekends and during leisure hours

---

## Recommendations
- Promote weekend and leisure-focused membership options
- Offer limited-time trial memberships to frequent casual riders
- Align marketing campaigns with peak casual rider usage times
